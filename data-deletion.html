User Data Deletion Instructions for AI Sales Agent

Last updated: September 25, 2026

AI Sales Agent is an educational and testing WhatsApp automation project.

How to Request Deletion

Email muhammadhusnainraza33@gmail.com with the subject AI Sales Agent - Data Deletion Request.

Include the WhatsApp phone number used to contact the AI Sales Agent and a clear request asking for your data to be deleted. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive credentials.

What Will Be Deleted

  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Name or profile name
  • Message content
  • Message identifiers and timestamps
  • Lead or customer information created by the test workflow

Processing Time

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled as soon as reasonably practical after the requester can be identified and the relevant data can be located. Some limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.

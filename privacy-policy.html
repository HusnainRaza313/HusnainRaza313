Privacy Policy for AI Sales Agent

Last updated: September 25, 2026

AI Sales Agent is an educational and testing project that demonstrates an AI-powered WhatsApp sales assistant using Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and n8n automation.

Information We Process

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your name or profile name, when provided by WhatsApp
  • The text of messages you send
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery/status information
  • Product or service interests that you choose to share

How We Use Information

  • Receiving and responding to WhatsApp messages
  • Understanding customer questions
  • Providing requested information
  • Qualifying leads for demonstration purposes
  • Testing automation workflows and integrations

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers required for the project to work, such as Meta/WhatsApp, n8n, and AI or data-storage services configured for the project.

Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for testing, troubleshooting, demonstration, or legal/security purposes. Test data may be deleted periodically.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

Your Choices

You may stop interacting with the AI Sales Agent at any time. You may also request deletion of personal information associated with your interactions.

# User Data Deletion Instructions for AI Sales Agent

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

AI Sales Agent is an educational and testing WhatsApp automation project.

If you have interacted with the AI Sales Agent and want your personal data deleted, you can request deletion using the steps below.

## How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

**muhammadhusnainraza33@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**AI Sales Agent - Data Deletion Request**

In your email, include:

- The WhatsApp phone number used to contact the AI Sales Agent
- A clear request asking for your data to be deleted

Do not include passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive credentials.

## What Will Be Deleted

Where applicable and reasonably identifiable, the deletion request may cover:

- WhatsApp phone number
- Name or profile name
- Message content
- Message identifiers and timestamps
- Lead or customer information created by the test workflow

## Processing Time

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled as soon as reasonably practical after the requester can be identified and the relevant data can be located.

Some limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.
